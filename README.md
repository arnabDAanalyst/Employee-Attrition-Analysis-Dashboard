# Key KPIs 
1️⃣   Overall Attrition Rate
Definition: Percentage of employees who left the company
Formula:
Attrition Rate = (Employees with Attrition = "Yes" ÷ Total Employees) × 100
Why it matters:
Helps understand overall employee retention health.

2️⃣   Average Monthly Income
Definition: Mean monthly salary of employees
Field Used: MonthlyIncome
Why it matters:
Used to analyze whether compensation impacts attrition.

3️⃣   Average Years at Company
Definition: Average employee tenure
Field Used: YearsAtCompany
Why it matters:
Indicates workforce stability and employee loyalty.

4️⃣   Overtime Attrition Rate
Definition: Attrition rate among employees who work overtime
Fields Used: OverTime, Attrition
Why it matters:
Shows whether workload stress contributes to attrition.

5️⃣   Department-wise Attrition Count
Definition: Number of employees leaving by department
Fields Used: Department, Attrition
Why it matters:
Identifies high-risk departments needing intervention.

# 📊 Business Analysis Questions (Simple to Moderate)

1️⃣   Which department has the highest employee attrition?
👉 Helps HR focus retention strategies department-wise.

2️⃣   Is attrition higher among employees who work overtime?
👉 Evaluates impact of workload and work-life balance.

3️⃣   Do employees with lower monthly income leave more frequently?
👉 Assesses compensation fairness and motivation.

4️⃣   Are new employees more likely to leave than experienced employees?
👉 Compare attrition by YearsAtCompany.

5️⃣   Does job satisfaction influence employee attrition?
👉 Analyze attrition across different JobSatisfaction levels.
